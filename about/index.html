We carry proven products into the world’s most complex growth market

Times Bridge US is the investment and partnership arm of The Times of India Group in the United States. We work at one specific seam in the global economy: the distance between a product that already works in America and an Indian enterprise that already needs it.

That distance is not technical. It is a matter of who you know, who vouches for you, and who is willing to do the unglamorous work of proving value inside a customer’s environment before a contract exists. We do that work. We source and diligence companies in the US market, we invest in the ones we believe in, we open the rooms in India that a foreign founder cannot open alone, and we stay accountable through deployment and expansion.

We work with founders and operators who treat India as central to their mission rather than as a line item, a market to scale across, monetise in and build from. In return we bring more than an introduction: capital, market strategy, local partnerships, delivery on the ground, and the patience that a market of India’s complexity demands.

“Our aspiration is to help our partners see India differently and, in so doing, see the world differently. We’re proud of the ideas we’ve helped lift off across India, and the many lessons our partners and India have taught us.”
Rishi JaitlyFounding CEO, Times Bridge

Our heritage

The Times of India Group

Founded in 1838, The Times of India Group is South Asia’s oldest media company and now its largest digital one. It publishes the world’s largest English-language newspaper, stages the awards that India watches, and holds leading positions in out-of-home media, radio and television.

Online, Times Internet reaches hundreds of millions of Indians every month and ranks first in the country across categories as varied as news, music, streaming, real estate and sport. For a partner entering India, that is not brand marketing, it is a standing invitation into the national conversation.
