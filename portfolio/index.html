The next corridor

Where the next bridges are being built

The pattern that worked for consumer platforms now applies to enterprise software and AI. These are the sector plays we are actively mapping, the buyer, the blocker and the sequence we run.

Financial services

Risk and decisioning software into India’s private banks

A product proven with American lenders cannot get a regulated Indian bank to evaluate it: no local reference customer, no relationship above the analyst level, and genuine sensitivity about data.

  • Open the conversation where technology budgets are actually decided
  • Scope a ring-fenced pilot on the bank’s own data, handling residency and compliance in country
  • Measure the result against the incumbent and produce a board-ready case
  • Structure the contract and use the reference to open the next two banks

Retail and consumer

Personalisation and commerce software into retail majors

India’s largest retailers run their own technology teams and are pitched by everyone. Getting past procurement requires credibility and a use case tied to a margin problem they already own.

  • Identify retailers actively investing in the category and reach the digital leadership
  • Run a short pilot on a live catalogue, wired into the existing stack
  • Measure basket and conversion lift against a control cohort
  • Convert the pilot into a rollout and run the channel

Manufacturing

Vision-based quality inspection into Indian factories

Manufacturers want hard proof on their own lines before committing capital expenditure, plus engineering support on site that a remote vendor cannot provide.

  • Map component and electronics manufacturers under margin pressure and reach plant leadership
  • Stand the system up on a single production line as a paid proof of value
  • Tune to the plant’s own defect taxonomy and quantify scrap and rework savings
  • Expand line by line into a multi-plant agreement with local support

Healthcare

Clinical decision support into hospital networks

Clinical adoption needs clinician trust, local validation and integration with hospital systems, none of which can be earned from abroad, and all of which sit behind medical leadership relationships.

  • Open conversations with medical directors and network leadership
  • Integrate with the network’s systems and run a blinded validation study
  • Work alongside clinicians to tune workflow and document accuracy locally
  • Structure a network-wide agreement and manage the rollout city by city

Technology services and capability centres

Developer productivity software across engineering organisations

Selling to India’s largest employers of engineers means navigating procurement, security review and proving productivity at scale, a motion no early team can run remotely.

  • Target firms and capability centres with live developer-efficiency mandates
  • Run a controlled rollout with one squad, integrated with the toolchain and security policy
  • Measure cycle time and throughput against a matched control team
  • Negotiate enterprise-wide expansion and manage renewal and growth

How we get paid

Aligned, not transactional

We are not a reseller taking a margin on someone else’s product. On the bridges we build, we invest in the company, tie part of our upside to the India revenue milestones we help create, and earn on the business we originate through our channel.

That structure is deliberate. It means we lose if the India entry stalls, and it keeps us in the room after the first contract is signed, which is exactly when most market entries quietly fail.

The sector plays above are illustrative of the model and the sequence we run. They describe target motions and buyer profiles rather than specific prior engagements.
