Girl Effect

Read the story

In 2019, Girl Effect began its work in India with Chhaa Jaa ("Go Forth and Shine"), a digital brand which has harnessed media content to equip over 10 million girls to make well-informed choices about their health and relationships. During the pandemic, Chhaa Jaa ran a 3-month campaign to introduce girls to the concept of telemedicine, while making 74,000 connections between girls and information about vetted sexual and reproductive health services. Chhaa Jaa's success as a youth-centered digital solution has been highlighted as a best-practice case study in a new framework developed in 2020 by the World Health Organization and others.

Most recently, Girl Effect launched Bol Behen ("Speak, Sister"), a chatbot designed to discuss sensitive topics, on WhatsApp, further expanding its reach and accessibility for girls and young women. The chatbot has touched over 10 million adolescent girls across the country and has won the Global Digital Health Innovation Award for its impact.

Girl Effect is a creative non-profit empowering millions of girls across Africa and Asia to change their lives. Times Bridge has enabled Girl Effect's market partnerships, media and content collaborations, the creation of an India Advisory Board, and public advocacy initiatives.

Headspace

“While the scale and diversity of India is widely known, our opportunity to make a significant impact in the ancient home of meditation is unique. We are delighted to partner with Times Bridge and The Times of India Group to expand Headspace's reach in the Indian market.”
Richard Pierson
Co-founder and CEO, Headspace

Read the story

In 2020, Headspace entered India, the ancient home of meditation and where co-founder Andy Puddicombe got his start as a Buddhist monk. Making a positive mark on India's cutthroat university environment, Headspace has provided subscriptions for university students attending select IITs and IIMs. Headspace leaned further into India during the pandemic, offering free access to healthcare professionals to offset stress and burnout. In June 2020, Headspace concluded its Series C, having raised $215.9M over 10 rounds. In August 2021, Headspace merged with Ginger to form Headspace Health. Times Bridge has supported Headspace's ecosystem influence, government engagements, brand building, strategic partnerships and market intelligence.
